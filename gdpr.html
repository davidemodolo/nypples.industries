Your Data Rights

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all data processed through your account within 30 days.
  • Right to Deletion: We permanently delete all your prompts, completions, and embeddings upon request. No retention beyond what you specify.
  • Right to Portability: Export all your data in machine-readable JSON format. No proprietary lock-in.
